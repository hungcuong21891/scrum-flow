Pre-planning

Once per sprint · same day as Grooming · 1 day before Planning
Driver: Unity Lead Contributor: DEV

New cadence — the bottoms-up estimation step that stops estimation leaking into Review / Planning.

Outcome (checklist)
Do what
Issue to fix
Before Planning

Polished PRD

  • Finalize PRD, escalate more with assigned DEV if needed
Issue to fix
  • PO & DEV must clear definition of done, acceptance criteria

Bottoms up from DEV

  • risks, blockers, etc.
  • checklist
  • estimation
  • timeline
  • Review PRD, code, etc. and follow up with PO to raise any risks, blockers, concerns, etc.
  • Breakdown implementation checklists and input estimation.
    • Important if a task is more than 8h, it must be broken into items of minimum 4h or 8h
    • Include a separate item called Verify, buffered for fixing bugs
  • Input the temporary Due date (DEV / DONE)
  • Plan your non-scrum time (day-off, meeting, learning, company event, fixed team meetings, planning, personal plan, review code, learning time)
  • Buffer adhoc issues (investigate issues)
  • Assign & buffer main branch merger
  • Submit your plan to PO, Unity Lead
  • PO, Unity Lead review (any mis-understanding, mis-estimation, etc.), escalate tasks that lack resources
Issue to fix
  • Most DEV mis-estimation root causes
